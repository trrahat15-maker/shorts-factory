Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Shorts Factory Daily ("the app") is a personal automation tool operated by its owner. This policy explains how the app handles data.

1. Information the app accesses

When you authorize the app through Google OAuth, the app requests access to your YouTube account for the following purposes:

  • Uploading videos to your own YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Reading basic channel information such as your channel name, video count, and subscriber count (youtube.readonly).

2. How data is used

Data is used solely to generate and upload video content to the authorized YouTube channel. No content is sold or shared with third parties.

3. Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely as encrypted secrets in the app owner's private environment (or GitHub repository secrets) and are used only to authenticate with the YouTube API.

4. Data sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third party.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at your Google Account permissions page.

6. Contact

For any questions, contact the app owner at the support email provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
